# PC MINER: CASES & HARDWARE - Game Design Document (GDD)

## 1. Concept
PC MINER: CASES & HARDWARE is a browser-based incremental game where players build, upgrade, and manage mining PCs. The game focuses on a collection-based progression with a high-quality iOS-inspired UI.

## 2. Core Loop
1. **Mine**: Earn coins over time based on PC power.
2. **Collect**: Claim accumulated coins from the storage.
3. **Upgrade**: Buy better components (CPU, GPU, RAM, etc.) in the Workshop or find them in Cases.
4. **Expand**: Open Cases to get rare hardware and new PC cases.
5. **Manage**: Optimize multiple PCs in the Garage.

## 3. UI/UX Style
- **Style**: iOS Liquid Glass.
- **Background**: #f2f2f7 (Light) / #000000 (Dark).
- **Cards**: White with 16px border-radius, backdrop-filter: blur(20px).
- **Typography**: System fonts (SF Pro, Inter, sans-serif).
- **Interactions**: Springy animations, haptic-like visual feedback.

## 4. Key Sections
- **Garage**: Overview of mining income and active PCs.
- **Cases**: Loot boxes with hardware and rare cases.
- **Workshop**: UI for upgrading the current PC's components.
- **Inventory**: Storage for all collected hardware.
- **Menu**: Settings and game management.

## 5. Progression & Balance
- **Currency**: Coins (Primary).
- **Hardware Tiers**: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary.
- **Mining Power**: Calculated based on the combination of components.
- **Storage Limit**: Mining income fills up over time and must be collected.

## 6. Technical Stack
- HTML5, CSS3 (Liquid Glass), Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).
- LocalStorage for data persistence.
- SVG/CSS graphics for visualization.

## Stage 3 – Mining System & Economy

### Mining Currency
- **BYTE** – primary resource generated by computers.
- Mining rate (BYTE/sec) = `totalPower * 0.1` where `totalPower` sums power of all installed components across all computers.
- Hourly income = `miningRate * 3600`.

### Storage
- Max storage = `miningRate * 3600 * 6` (six hours of production).
- Storage fills over time; when full, mining pauses until player clicks **Забрать**.
- Collection moves stored BYTE to the player's BYTE balance and resets storage.

### Offline Progress
- On game load, elapsed real‑time since `lastUpdate` is computed.
- Earned BYTE = `min(elapsedSec, timeToFull) * miningRate` where `timeToFull = maxStorage / miningRate`.
- No extra BYTE beyond storage capacity.

### Exchange

**Exchange Rate**
- `exchangeRate` – количество BYTE, требуемое для получения 1 COIN. По умолчанию `1000`.
- Курc отображается в гараже и на странице обмена.
- При обмене проверяется, что у игрока достаточно BYTE, и получаемое количество COIN рассчитывается как `Math.floor(amount / exchangeRate)`.

- Players can exchange BYTE for **COIN** at a configurable rate (`exchangeRate` bytes per 1 COIN).
- Exchange history is displayed in the Exchange screen.
- Each exchange grants a small amount of experience.

### Experience & Levels
- Actions (case opening, component installation, exchanges) award experience.
- Level‑up when `exp >= level * 1000`; level increases and exp resets.
- Simple alerts inform the player of level‑up.

### Achievements (example)
- *Первый майнинг* – collect any BYTE.
- *1 000 BYTE* – accumulate at least 1 000 BYTE.
